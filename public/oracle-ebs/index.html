YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Oracle EBS staffing FAQs

Need something more specific? support@ething.in ↗

01What Oracle E-Business Suite roles can we discuss?

Share requirements for Financials, SCM, manufacturing or HRMS functional consultants, EBS technical developers, Apps DBAs and testing or support specialists. Availability and screening criteria are confirmed against your brief.

02How does eThing approach ERP staffing?

Our ERP staffing discussion starts with your platform, modules, role scope and required experience. For Oracle EBS teams, discuss contract staffing, permanent hiring or team augmentation, with working arrangements and responsibilities agreed before selection.

03Can you help us staff an Oracle EBS implementation or upgrade?

Discuss the workstreams and expertise your programme requires. The team structure, candidate availability and start dates are agreed after reviewing your modules, release, customisations and testing needs.

04Is Oracle E-Business Suite the same as Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP?

They are distinct Oracle application suites. This page focuses on E-Business Suite. Specify EBS experience, modules and release in your hiring brief so that relevant skills are assessed.

05Can consultants work onsite, remotely or in a hybrid arrangement?

Share location, working hours, access rules and any onsite requirements. The suitable arrangement depends on the role, candidate availability and your programme’s needs.

06How are consultants assessed?

Agree the screening approach before sourcing. Functional assessment can cover module configuration and business-process scenarios; technical assessment can cover PL/SQL, interfaces and troubleshooting. Your team reviews profiles, interviews candidates and agrees any practical assessment needed.

07Does staffing include ownership of delivery or production support?

Responsibilities depend on the engagement. This page focuses on specialists joining your team. Project ownership, support hours, response commitments and change authority require an explicitly agreed scope.

08What affects pricing and the start date?

Module depth, experience, location, engagement length and team size affect pricing. Start dates also depend on interviews, candidate availability and onboarding. Both are agreed for your requirement.

09What experience should we specify for Oracle Apps DBA roles?

For Oracle Apps DBA hiring, specify your Oracle EBS release, application tier, database responsibilities, cloning and patching requirements. Agree how the consultant’s relevant environment experience will be assessed.

10Do you provide Oracle consulting services or staffing?

This page focuses on hiring specialists for your EBS team. If you need Oracle consulting services with defined deliverables, share the scope for assessment. Project ownership, acceptance criteria and support responsibilities must be agreed separately from staffing.

11Can we hire one consultant or a dedicated team?

Discuss a single specialist or a coordinated group of roles. Oracle EBS staff augmentation adds people to the team you manage. Dedicated-team availability, team leadership, reporting and delivery responsibilities must be agreed for your scope.

12Can consultants work alongside our implementation partner?

Include your implementation partner’s responsibilities in the brief. Agree who assigns work, approves designs, manages system access and accepts deliverables so consultants can work within the project structure.

13What onboarding and replacement support is included?

Agree documentation, access approvals, induction, reporting and handover responsibilities before the start date. Any replacement arrangement, eligibility, timing or charges must be confirmed in the engagement terms; no automatic replacement guarantee is implied.

14Are contract-to-hire and remote arrangements available?

Ask about Oracle EBS contract staffing, contract-to-hire or remote Oracle EBS consultants. Options depend on role and candidate availability, location, access requirements and agreed terms. For contract-to-hire, confirm the assessment period, conversion conditions and any fees in advance.
