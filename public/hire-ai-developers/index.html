Plan your next AI hire with confidence.

The process, engagement and ownership details to discuss before you start.

01How quickly can I receive AI developer profiles?+

Our shortlist target for AI developers is within 48 hours after we understand your requirements. Timing depends on the specialist role, candidate availability and your interview process; the engineer’s start date is agreed separately.

02Can I interview AI engineers before hiring?+

Yes. Review profiles and interview AI developers on your shortlist to assess technical decisions, communication and fit with your team before choosing an engagement.

03Can you add GenAI and LLM features to our existing product?+

Yes. Share your architecture, data sources, evaluation needs and deployment environment. We can match an ML developer or a GenAI specialist to your application and integration work.

04How do engineers work with our internal team?+

Through staff augmentation, engineers work in your repositories, code reviews and sprint process. You retain daily technical direction and delivery priorities, while eThing supports the engagement.

05Can I hire one specialist, a team or for a short-term project?+

You can hire AI developers individually, as a dedicated team or through contract staffing for an agreed project period. The proposal sets out skills, duration, collaboration hours and candidate availability.

06Can we scale the team, and what happens if the fit is not right?+

Discuss changes in team size as your roadmap evolves. Replacement support, notice periods and any associated terms are agreed in your engagement, so expectations are clear before work starts.

07How much does an AI engineering engagement cost?+

Pricing depends on seniority, specialist skills, team size, collaboration hours and duration. Share your requirements for a tailored staffing solution. Scope and commercial terms are agreed before onboarding.

08How do you handle IP, confidentiality and data access?+

We agree confidentiality, intellectual property, permitted tools and data access during contracting. Your team defines access permissions and any restrictions on external model providers. Required agreements and security onboarding are completed before engineers access your systems.

09How does staff augmentation compare with AI development companies?+

Many artificial intelligence development companies manage a defined project and its delivery. With an embedded engineer, your team retains technical direction. We clarify scope, delivery responsibilities and ownership before proposing an engagement.
