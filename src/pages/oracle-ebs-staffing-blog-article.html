Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) runs core business operations such as finance, procurement, inventory, supply chain, and order management. Keeping it dependable takes specialists for implementation, customization, integrations, upgrades, and daily support, and recruiting every one of them locally is slow and costly. That is why many companies now look at offshore oracle staffing services. Oracle states that Premier Support for EBS 12.2 continues through at least 2037, so these skills stay relevant. What Are Oracle Staffing Services?

For Oracle E-Business Suite, these services supply experienced professionals who join your ERP team. Typical uses include:

  • Hiring functional consultants and technical consultants.
  • Adding specialists to an existing ERP team.
  • Providing resources for implementation and upgrade projects.
  • Supporting application maintenance and troubleshooting.
  • Recruiting for short-term and long-term requirements.

Staffing differs from project-based consulting. With staffing, professionals contribute to your team while you direct priorities and own delivery. With project-based consulting, the provider may take responsibility for defined deliverables. Companies that want to keep control of the work often choose IT staff augmentation.

Benefits depend on each consultant's skills, the project scope, communication, and the engagement model. Savings are not guaranteed, and offshore consultants do not all have the same experience, so assess every profile individually.

Types of Oracle EBS Consultants You Can Hire

Most businesses need a particular skill set, not a general ERP resource. Start by separating the roles.

Oracle EBS Functional Consultant

An Oracle EBS functional consultant understands business processes and configures EBS applications to meet organizational requirements. Responsibilities include gathering business requirements, analyzing existing workflows, configuring modules, supporting functional testing and UAT, preparing documentation, coordinating with technical teams, and resolving user issues. Relevant modules include Oracle Financials (GL, AP, AR, and Fixed Assets), Purchasing, Inventory Management, Order Management, and Oracle Supply Chain Management (SCM).

Oracle EBS Technical Consultant

A technical consultant works on development, customization, integrations, reports, and troubleshooting. Typical tasks include PL/SQL development, Oracle Forms and Reports, Oracle Workflow, data migration, API development, concurrent programs, and performance improvements. Much of this work follows the RICEW components: Reports, Interfaces, Conversions, Extensions, and Workflows.

Oracle EBS Techno-Functional Consultant

A techno-functional consultant combines business knowledge with technical understanding. This profile suits projects that need coordination between business users, functional teams and developers.

Key Skills to Look for When Hiring Oracle EBS Consultants

Skills matter when they tie to real EBS work, so ask how each one was used.

Oracle EBS Module Knowledge

Match module knowledge to your environment: Financials, Procurement, Inventory, Order Management and Supply Chain, plus Human Capital Management where relevant. A consultant who knows your modules can spot where processes connect across them.

Technical Skills

PL/SQL and SQL underpin most customization and data work. Oracle Forms, Reports, and XML or BI Publisher shape screens and outputs. Workflow, concurrent programs, and APIs automate processes and connect systems, while RICEW experience shows a consultant can deliver complete custom components.

Functional and Business Skills

Business process analysis, requirement gathering, and module configuration turn needs into working setups. Functional testing, user acceptance testing, documentation, and end-user support keep changes usable.

Communication and Problem-Solving

Offshore consultants must communicate clearly, document issues, collaborate remotely, and coordinate with your internal ERP team. Unclear handovers cause costly rework.

What Oracle EBS Support Services Do Businesses Commonly Need?

Ongoing oracle ebs support services often cover:

  • Application maintenance and troubleshooting.
  • Functional and technical issue resolution.
  • Report and interface maintenance.
  • Performance and workflow troubleshooting.
  • User support and documentation.
  • Customization and integration maintenance.
  • Patch and upgrade-related technical assistance.

Responsibilities depend on your EBS version, modules, customizations, infrastructure, and support agreement. Third-party consultant staffing is not the same as Oracle's official software support. Hiring an offshore consultant does not replace your Oracle support entitlements.

How Does Offshore Oracle EBS Staffing Work?

Step 1: Identify Your Oracle EBS Requirements

Define the modules, technologies, experience levels, and project responsibilities, plus your EBS release.

Step 2: Select the Right Consultant Profile

Decide whether you need a functional, technical, or techno-functional consultant.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Oracle ERP staffing involve?

It supplies experienced Oracle EBS professionals, functional, technical, or both, who join your team for implementation, upgrade, or support work. You direct priorities while the provider handles sourcing and screening. It differs from project-based consulting, where the provider owns defined deliverables.

How can I hire Oracle EBS consultants from India?

Define your modules, release and role scope, then choose a functional, technical or techno-functional profile. Shortlist candidates, run scenario-based interviews, agree the engagement model, and onboard with documented access and reporting. A staffing partner can source and screen while you make the final selection.

What is the difference between an Oracle EBS functional and technical consultant?

A functional consultant focuses on business processes, requirements, and module configuration. A technical consultant builds and fixes reports, interfaces, customizations, and integrations using PL/SQL and related tools. Many projects need both, and a techno-functional consultant can bridge the two.

What skills should an Oracle EBS technical consultant have?

Look for PL/SQL and SQL, Oracle Forms and Reports, Workflow, XML or BI Publisher, concurrent programs, and APIs. RICEW experience matters, as does troubleshooting and performance tuning. Ask for examples of interfaces or conversions they personally built in a comparable environment.

What does a functional consultant do in Oracle EBS?

They gather business requirements, analyze workflows, configure modules such as financials, purchasing, or inventory, support testing and UAT, write documentation, and resolve user issues. They translate business needs into system setup and work closely with technical consultants.

Can I hire offshore Oracle EBS consultants for ongoing support?

Yes, but responsibilities depend on the engagement. Staffing adds people to your team, so support hours, response expectations, and change authority must be agreed in writing. It does not replace Oracle software support entitlements.

How much does it cost to hire Oracle EBS consultants in India?

There is no fixed price. Cost depends on experience, role, module depth, release knowledge, engagement duration, and team size. Ask providers for a written quote based on your requirements, and confirm what it includes before you commit.

How does Oracle EBS staffing differ from ongoing application support?

Staffing supplies consultants who work inside your team under your direction. Application support is an agreed service covering defined tasks, hours, and responsibilities. Oracle also provides official software support separately, covering its own updates and patches.

Can I hire Oracle consultants for short-term projects?

Yes. Contract engagements suit rollouts, upgrades, interface builds, or temporary support gaps. Agree on scope, duration, deliverables, and handover documentation up front so your team can maintain the work once the consultant leaves.

How do I choose the right Oracle provider?

Check Oracle EBS recruitment experience, both functional and technical profiles, screening methods, module-specific project history, transparent terms, time-zone fit, onboarding support, and replacement policy. Interview candidates yourself and test them on realistic scenarios.
