## Intent note

“Need website developer” may bring website-project buyers rather than staff-augmentation buyers. It is mapped to the software-development service and distinguished from developer hiring. “Hire DevOps developer” is explained in terms of automation, deployment and platform responsibilities. Generic staff-augmentation queries are not claimed to be exclusively commercial.
